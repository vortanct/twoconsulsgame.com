# Two Consuls

A co-op strategy game for two, set on the Roman frontier in the 1st–2nd century AD.

Rome never trusted one man with power, so it elected two consuls. In Two Consuls you and a friend are those two: you walk the edge of the empire in Germania, build a legion camp by day with the coins you carry, and hold it against raiders by night.

## How it plays

- **Day.** Walk left and right along the frontier and drop coins where they matter: hire refugees, hand out tools to turn them into legionaries, archers, builders and farmers, order walls and towers.
- **Evening.** A horn sounds and units fall back to the walls.
- **Night.** Raiders come out of the forest to steal coins and break walls. Hold until dawn.
- Each night is harder than the last. Survive five and finish the road to the outpost to win.

## What makes it Roman

- **Two consuls, one treasury.** Each of you carries a purse, but the big decisions, like a stone wall or the outpost, need coins from both.
- **Roads.** Lay a via from the camp to the outpost. Everyone on it moves faster, and once it is finished, supplies start to arrive.
- **Formations.** Raise shields into a testudo when slingers come, or open into a line to push back a charge.
- **Romanization.** Not every village has to burn. Bring gifts three days running and its raiders become allies.

Play online, or side by side on one screen. Solo works too.
