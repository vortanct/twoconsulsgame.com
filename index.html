Two Consuls

A co-op strategy game for two. Build a legion camp on the Roman frontier by day, and hold it against raiders by night.

Follow the devlog Get updates

Rome sent two of you

The Republic never trusted one man with power, so it elected two consuls. Here, that is the whole game: you and a friend walk the edge of the empire, spend coins on soldiers, walls and roads, and argue about what comes first before the horn sounds.

Play online, or side by side on one screen. Solo works too.

What makes it Roman

Two consuls, one treasury

Each of you carries a purse, but the big decisions, like a stone wall or the outpost, need coins from both.

Roads

Lay a via from the camp to the outpost. Everyone on it moves faster, and once it is finished, supplies start to arrive.

Formations

Raise shields into a testudo when slingers come, or open into a line to push back a charge. Choosing wrong costs you the wall.

Romanization

Not every village has to burn. Bring gifts three days running and its raiders become allies. Miss a day and the next night is worse.

Where the road has reached

Two Consuls is made by one developer in the evenings, with Godot. Right now the camp is coloured rectangles; the plan is a playable demo next year.
